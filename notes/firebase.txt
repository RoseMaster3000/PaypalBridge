
Setting Up Firebase:
(replace <PROJECT> with your project ID, mine was bridge-fdc2a)\

[1] GENERATE FirebaseKey.json
https://console.firebase.google.com/u/0/project/<PROJECT>/settings/serviceaccounts/adminsdk

[2] ENABLE API
https://console.developers.google.com/apis/api/firestore.googleapis.com/overview?project=<PROJECT>

[3] CREATE DATASTORE (NATIVE)
https://console.cloud.google.com/datastore/setup?project=<PROJECT>
