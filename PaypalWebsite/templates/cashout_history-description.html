• 70/30 Split System — Revenue is split before fees: 70% to the player, 30% to the developer.
  • Time — When the cashout was created.
  • Gems — Number of gems redeemed for this payout.
  • Total Payout — Amount allocated to the player before PayPal fee.
  • User Payout — Amount the player receives after PayPal fee.
  • PayPal Fee — Fee deducted from the player’s share, not yours.
  • Admin Profit — Your 30% share of total revenue (your real profit).
  • Note: Real PayPal fees may vary slightly depending on country and currency.
